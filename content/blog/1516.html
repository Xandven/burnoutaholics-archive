CG has released info on the Toy pack.

$12.99 for all 9, 1000 MS points! Is my 13 bucks worth of cars, and bike, going to keep me satisfied until the boost specials come out? when I am expected to then shell out some more MS points for those, probably 800 , and then the island, God knows what that update will cost.Now I know that all of the other updates were free, and business is business, add that all up, and you have now spent around 80 dollars, including the game.Some will argue that the game is new every time you update it.It is somewhat new.How about giving me a free car when I purchase the whole pack (well I guess the bike counts as a free something).At least making me feel like I got a little bonus for spending my hard earned cash, or if you buy 7 you can win 2 more by beating their burning routes, and you have to shut them down, that was fun stuff.Give me a beater that you just can't repair you have to put some miles on it, and win some races with it in order to fix it up a little each time, or upgrade it.Come on CG lets see some imagination, and creativeness when it comes to parting with our cash we want more bang for our bucks!!We need goals, something to shoot for, not just stuff we can buy, we want to win it!

Don't get me wrong, I love new stuff, and yes I will buy the whole pack, because after all, My name is Dodger455, and I am a Burnoutaholic!
