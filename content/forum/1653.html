Just posted this on the EA forums, thought I'd give it a try here as well...

Howdy,

First I'd just like to say how much I love the Party Play add-on. I've always been a fan of the old Burnout 2 take-turns crash events with friends (and foes), so the Party Play DLC for Paradise was one of the best things that could have happened to the game. However, now after plenty of hours spent in it, me and my friends are starting to see a certain pattern of repetition. It's still fun, I just wish there'd be more to it, at least coming in the future.

Say, for example, an added 'motorcycle' section for Party play, mixed down together with the old one (not like "You either pick Motorcycle or Car Party Play" but with both disciplines' events mixed into the same Party Play 'Random Cycle'). And with Big Surf coming now, do you think you'll be adding for example new insane Stunt Events for Party Play? If, of course, one's downloaded the Island. And perhaps, some Party Play competitions happening at night? Well, really, any additions to Party Play as a whole would be much appreciated. It's already awesome, but it could be so much more.

Also, one more thing. If you ever decide to update the Party interface, could you please add an option to more precisely select which events you want to race? Like not just "Random, Stunt or Time", but more exactly having perhaps a picture and the same describing text to every event so that you can handpick which ones to have a Party blast in?

Just a few ideas and wishes.
