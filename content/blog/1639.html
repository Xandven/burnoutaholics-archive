I have been an online gamer since I went to college and part of making a game, we also had to test out so many games on and offline so we can successfully create our own.  Lately I have been thinking about somethings and concerning this site, I'm beginning to wonder about some posts on here.  I liked the Burnout series bc when you went online, the people were friendlier and cooler than in other games and game genres. Yes playing Burnout is extremely crazy and violent in some aspect, still you find  mostly cool people on it. 

Now it seems, that even  in Burnout, and Burnoutaholics; poison is beginning to spill over. We have wise cracking people believing that are righting some sort of injustice against criterion, posting ridiculous posts with provactive titles (just like mine lol), all to get people talking and becoming more savage monsters than human beings. What happend to the bond that us burners shared. There is a reason why I don't join clans, even fraternities or any group.  Those reasons are begging to spill over onto this website.  Seems the maturity level on here is decreasing as time goes on.  When did us burner's become so vein and closed-minded, so rude to each other? Don't blame the game for talking about Takedowns and Revenge and Paybacks.  It's bad enough that the game is changing, you can either change with it or leave. I gaurantee those who do not like paradise, have not progressed to their full potential to see the pros over cons in the game is still worth it. I didn't like Paradise when I first played it either, but I gave it a chance like I have with all Burnout titles.

But this is what most online gamers, gamers in general are. Just people who live through their gaming bc for some odd reason, it's maybe better than the reality they live.  Why would someone join the site just to be rude, or to say that they hate the game lol. Pointless...

I guess I ignored what Burnoutaholics really meant.  What can be done to save this site?
