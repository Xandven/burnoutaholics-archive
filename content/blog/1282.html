Some of you already know that I have temporarily lost Xandu to the XNA Creators Studio. Briefly today he stepped out of his lab to show me what he has come up with so far.

He has made a beat recognition game, kinda like guitar hero with dropping coloured blocks to the beat of whatever music you have playing whilst explosions go on in the background... Although the smoke from the explosions does hide the coloured blocks which makes it a little difficult... it had me in stitches as the explosions were going off and I was bashing my triggers as best as I could only to see the word "Failed" float away from my coloured blocks until the screen was pretty much filled up with my repeated failures!! 😃 🎵

The next step is to get it to work with the Guitar and Drum kit!! 🥵
