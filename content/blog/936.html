Hi there,

I recently bough Paradise and i've got to say it is awesome, the graphics, the gameplay and sound are all top notch and the cars all handle and feel different, quite an acheivment for a game thats not a simulation like Forza Motorsport 2.

The smashes, billboards and jumps were something I really liked when I first switched on because it was different, now, having completed all 3, I can say that although frustrating, these were really good fun.

Have also acheived my burnout license and am well on the way to elite, 50 odd cars, and started playing online, really brilliant if people actually want to do challenges, because if they don't, it really ruins everyone's fun.

Maybe a patch could be added to note what is going on in the game so people joining can choose races, challenges or just run about.

Anyone looking to help online challenges, just add me and send me a message as i'm always looking for a good group to knock out some barrel rolls or huge jumps.

So thats my take on the game, hope everyone enjoys it as much as me, and one question, when do you get the F1 cars, seen them online but I really want one.

Thanks for reading.
