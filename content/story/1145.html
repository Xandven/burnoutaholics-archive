Criterion Games have released the Bike pack. It is available on the 18th of September for PS3 and Xbox 360.

Click HERE for the full story at criteriongames.com

The Bike pack is a FREE download of Game Changing Content. It includes 4 bikes, 70 new Freeburn Challenges, 38 Burning Rides, a Day/Night cycle and atmospheric weather conditions.

Only 2 of the bikes will be available to use straight away, we are not sure how or when the other 2 bikes will become available yet.

Criterion have also announced a new competition today on their Crash TV podcast episode 9. The first person to complete a Bike challenge online will get the opportunity to name something on the new Island, which will be released later this year (hopefully). So make sure you get downloading on the 18th of September for your chance to change Paradise City!
