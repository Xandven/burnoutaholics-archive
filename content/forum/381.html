I know not everybody watches TV, if it wasn't for Xbox I don't think Bootleg would bother having one and paying the licence fee. But if you know where to look there is some really interesting stuff out there.

The last few weeks we have been watching Riget on DVD. Riget (The Kingdom) is a Danish TV show which ran for 8 episodes in the 90's. Stephen King then adapted it for American audiences and it became "Kingdom Hospital". I haven't seen the american version but the Danish original is COOL! We only have one episode left to watch and I want to see the Stephen King version now just to see what is missing as I'm sure some of the most gruesome scenes will have been cut out.
