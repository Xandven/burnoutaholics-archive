At the moment the only game that makes me want to come back for more is Battlefield 1943. It has land, sea and air racing and also the odd kill or two. Probably the best first person shooter out on any games console since it can actually handle 24+ players in one game without lag. Which means that when you shoot someone in the head they actually die! It also has the arcady feel to it that makes games all that more fun.

Also it helps that grenades that you have thrown actually blows up after you die. Which is way more realistic than just removing the live grenade from gameplay that other more realistic FPSs do.

See you on Wake Island!
