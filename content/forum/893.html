I have found a good glitch that is pretty cool. Basically I can make my carbon car any colour I want, all you have to do is get your carbon car out, send an invite to a friend, drive into a junkyard, scroll over to a car that has the colour you want your carbon car to be, DO NOT PRESS ANYTHING, Once the game starts you will pop outside, to you your car will appear as the car that was highlighted but to everyone else a coloured version of the carbon car that you just had out.
To see the car yourself leave and join a new game and there you have it.
The car will stay that colour untill you switch cars or get out of the game.
I found this glitch out on my OWN and did NOT copy anyone.
