There have recently been some derogatory remarks towards Burnout and Criterion on the site. Although your Blogs are your own personal opinion and we will not edit them unless they contravene the terms of use of BurnoutAholics or use swear words, please remember that this is a Burnout fansite and if you are not a fan of the Burnout series you have no place here.

It has been brought to my attention that since we added addiction points the quality of the blogs and comments has deteriorated. It's about quality, not quantity! Keeping to the following guide lines should help to keep the site looking pretty.

Comments

  • Give your comment a Title. Do not use just a . or part of the body of the comment as this looks like you are repeating yourself. Remember that the Title is shown on the Recent Comments list so keep it interesting and relevant. If you can't think of a title call it RE: "the name of the blog/story".
  • Do not write single word or very short sentence comments, if you don't have anything of substance to add think twice before posting.
  • Do not Spam or start / take part in flame wars - BurnoutAholics takes this very seriously and you risk being banned if you abuse the site.

Blogs

  • Your Blog will be read by other BurnoutAholics so try to keep it interesting or entertaining for all.
  • If you want to spell check you can copy and paste into another program ie wordpad and spell check there.
  • Do not navigate or use the back button unless you have saved what you are writing or it will be lost.

Forum

  • You voted for a forum so use it! If you have a topic of discussion the forum is the place for it.
  • Any site suggestions including Poll topics should be posted in the forums.
  • Do not Spam or start / take part in flame wars - BurnoutAholics takes this very seriously and you risk being banned if you abuse the site.

We now have PM's up and running so private messages can be sent instead of being posted as blogs/comments.

Thank you for contributing to BurnoutAholics and making it the Best Burnout Fansite out there! 😃
