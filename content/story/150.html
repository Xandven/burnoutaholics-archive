Tracks

Burnout 3: Takedown has a total of 18 different tracks spread across three continents. Since all of them have reverse versions as well the total number of tracks is 36. You get to race on tracks in Europe, Far East and USA. Most of the tracks are inspired by real cities or places. The total number of race locations in Burnout 3 Takedown is 10. Three in the USA, 4 in Europe and 3 in the Far East. The rest of the tracks on each continent are combinations of these tracks. 

Most of the tracks in Burnout 3: Takedown are quite bright, colorful and sunny. As opposed to Burnout 2: Point of Impact there are no weather effects. 

Below you will find a list of all the tracks in Burnout 3 Takedown. We have also written a note about what real world city or area the track might be inspired by.
USA
Silver Lake
This track is probably inspired by the Rocky Mountain National Park.
Downtown
Inspired by Chicago. The track is in a major city with congested streets and tight alleyways. There are also elevated train supports to look out for.
Waterfront
Inspired by San Diego. Costal track with lots of traffic. You should watch out for the crossingø trams on this track.
Kings of the Road
Downtown - Waterfront - Silverlake.
Lakeside Getaway
Silver Lake - Waterfront
Mountain Parkway
Silver Lake - Downtown
Europe
Alpine
Inspired by Autobahn in Germany.
Vineyard
Probably inspired by Southern France.
Winter City
Snow covered track that is probably inspired by Vienna. Parts of this track have trams running on it.
Riviera
Most likely based on the Monte Carlo circuit in Monaco.
Coastal Dream
Riviera - Vineyard
Continental Run
Alpine - Riviera - Vineyard
Alpine Expressway
Winter City - Riviera
Frozen Peak
Winter City - Alpine
The Far East
Golden City
Based on Bangkok. This is a fast track with marketplaces, monorails and highways.
Dockside
Inspired by Hong Kong.
Island Paradise
A very scenic track probably inspired by Koh Samui.
Tropical Drive
Island Paradise - Golden City

 

Cars

Burnout 3: Takedown has 7 car classes:

  • Compact Series
  • Muscle Series
  • Coupe Series
  • Sports Series
  • Super Series
  • Special Series
  • Heavy Weight Series

There are 10 cars in each series except Special and Heavy Weight:

  • Type 1, Type 2, Type 3
  • Tuned
  • Modified
  • Custom
  • Assassin
  • Prototype
  • DX
  • Dominator

The Special Series has 6 cars:

  • Oval Racer Special
  • Custom Coupe Ultimate
  • Euro Circuit Racer
  • Classic Hotrod
  • US Circuit Racer
  • World Circuit Racer

The Heavy Weight Series has 11 cars:

  • Heavy Pick-up
  • 4wd Racer
  • SUV Deluxe
  • 4WD Heavy Duty
  • B-Team Van
  • Delivery Truck
  • Tractor Cab
  • Longnose Cab
  • City Bus
  • Trash Truck
  • Fire Truck

Any car can be used for crash events. Heavy Weights can only be used in crash events.
