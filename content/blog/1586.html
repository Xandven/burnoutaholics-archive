This subject has been bugging me ever since CG announced the Party Pack achievements and trophy lists, then the DLC cars came out with trophies for PS3 but no achievements for Xbox 360.

Now, we know Cops and Robbers has 8 trophies for PS3 and only 3 Xbox 360 Achievements.

Criterion Games line on this is that Microsoft limit the number of achievements they can use within a game, this comes from the FAQ over at CriterionGames.com;

How come there are new PlayStation 3 Trophies but no new Xbox360 Achievements?
 
Microsoft put a limit on the number of Achievements a game can award. Sony don't put the same limitation on Trophies.
 

I have since been trying to find out what this limit is, with limited success. I know there is a limit on the amount of GamerScore available and have read the official rules for achievements over at the GamerScore Blog HERE but there is no mention of a limit of number of Achievements a game can award.

So, what is the limit?

I have looked at past games to see what the maximum amount has been previously, and The Orange Box has 99 different achievements. This would make sense as the upper limit as you can only have 100 friends on your list, 100 recent players, so having the same amount (or very close too) for the achievement list makes sense.

Some people think that as Orange Box is a special case, being more than one game in the box, that they were given special priviledges. But, this is the only guide I have to go on as to what a maximum number of Achievements allowed is.

Pretty much all of the other talk about achievements focus's on the amount of gamerscore available, not the actual number of different Achievements.

I'm sure I read somewhere that there is no limit to the number of achievements, only to the amount of gamerscore, and the gamerscore blog would certainly seem to indicate that this is the case. But, if there is no limit (or if the limit is 99) why doesn't Burnout Paradise for the 360 have equivalent Achievements for ALL of the PS3 Trophies in DLC?

Until I get an answer, or until the Island is announced with a minimal amount of Achievements for stupidly high gamerscore points, I will let it go.. but if anybody has any actual facts about the limits for the number of different Achievements available for CG to use for DLC please let me know! 🙂
