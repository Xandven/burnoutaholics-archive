Burnout Revenge?????

If i have all the time, i'd wanna play my favorite game everyday - Burnout Revenge..... I know i am not that good 'n fast, but, i just love to play it, especially, online. I know you know what i mean, the fun feeling of playing with your friends 'n nemeses racing 'n taking 'em down accidentally or intentionally or purely road rage just to get everyone out of your way. NICE!!!!!

Only that sometimes when i get on to play, most of the time, i don't see that many racers playing this game anymore. These days, it's not so often to see the lobby filled with racers, pros, joes 'n noobs just like me: your one 'n only 'ProfessionlNoob'. Anyways, what i'm trying to say is that Burnout Revenge is fading..... or, should i say dying????? Sometimes, i just can't stop to wonder why there wasn't any add-ons to this awesome game. Why did they make add-ons to the Paradise but not to the Revenge? I have heard of the tournaments they held with the Revenge game, meaning, this game isn't just that good but awesomely great!!!!! And, i still see some old school racers back here to play 'n have some fun; plus, there's more 'n more newcomers joining in to play the game 'n eager to learn the secrets of Burnout Revenge. The gamers, i meant, all of them, e.g. NOOBS - the newcomers, OLD-SCHOOLERS - the Burnoutaholics, PROs - rank racers 'n lap timers, JOEs - fun racers,'n don't forget the road rage hotheads 'n crash maniacs, they all play surely to have fun 'n to keep the game alive. But!!!!! after all that, where are the track add-ons that we've all been waiting for?????
