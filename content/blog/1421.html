The best incarnation of online Road Rage IMO is in Burnout 3: Takedown. It had all the right ingredients, and the takedown physics were spot on. I loved nothing more then being on the Blue team in my Super Assassin, running as fast as I can away from a horde of Red Euro Circuit Racers.

In Paradise, it's not quite the same.

Instead of all racers starting in the same place, we all start wherever we are on the map.

Instead of the blue team earning boost and the red team having infinite boost, the blue team has not boost at all.

Instead of all going in the same direction, you can go wherever you like, the blue team can take many different routes to get to the check points and finishing line.

It is no longer a game of chase, but it is more like a game of cat and mouse, Road Rage in Paradise is not the high speed intense close battle that it was in Burnout 3: Takedown.

This could easily be changed, with 4 simple steps:

1) Everybody starts in the same place, on a starting line.

2) The Blue Team gets a few seconds headstart.

3) The Blue Team can earn boost, Red keeps infinite boost.

4) We are all heading for the same destination in the same direction.

With these changes I would play Road Rage a lot more then I do now in Burnout Paradise.
