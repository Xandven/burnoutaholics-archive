I have taken quite a liking to the toy cars, and as I am driving through Paradise demolishing every car that gets in my way, I can't help but wonder why we can't put in for some extreme toys.By extreme, I mean like the Extreme Hotrod as a toy, or lets get some toy upgrades, like superchargers sticking out of the hood, and a little more speed, and awesome locked on boost.Why we are at it, lets have a drag bike, or a toy chopper.I would have liked to have seen an old drag strip in Paradise, that would have been cool to race in,I mean they have the oval dirt track right?As the game gets older, and we get bored of it, we need some wicked ass rides to mix it up.How many barrel rolls, and flat spins can we do in the Extreme Hotrod before we go mad!!I have switched to toys for glitched barrel rolls, and flatspins, because it is more fun, and harder to do.By the way who knew the Toy Citizen has two different sirens?, the switch is different than the big brother, just figured that one out, I felt like a noob, and not only that,I just found out that the bikes go faster when you lean forward, and the rider pulls in arms, and legs!Well yeah he goes faster!What other stuff don't I know?
