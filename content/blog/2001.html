We are still making changes to the site after the major update we did a couple of weeks ago.

The following has been changed / fixed:

  • FAQs are back online and all links to FAQs should be working again.
  • Added Burnout CRASH! FAQ.
  • Added automated moderation to all posts. This is to stop spam posters. The system has been running for 3 weeks and so far 25 spam posts have been stopped. Only one spam post made it through which means that over 96% of the spam attempts were automatically stopped. If you post a blog or comment and it does not show up on the site it means that it was probably flagged as spam. If you did nothing wrong you have nothing to fear and the post will show up on the site within a couple of days. If the content was actual spam your user will be deleted and email/username reported to our spam detection service. We enforce a zero tolerance policy on spam and there are no 2nd chances!
