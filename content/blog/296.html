New Paradise info available on 1up.com. Alex Ward talks about how Paradise online is revolutionary with no lobbies, no waiting, a seamless experience and focus on co-operative and social gameplay with lots of simple and fun challenges that groups of players can do together.

Eurogamer also has a Paradise preview where they mention the hand brake and Power Parking. They also go into the changes to Crash mode a little and do a nice round up of everything we already know about Paradise. It ends with a clever Joni Mitchell lyric parody.

CVG also have a preview online and they sound like they are loving it!
