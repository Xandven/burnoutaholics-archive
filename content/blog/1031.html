As an Elite poster on the forums I and the other Elite posters have been awarded a special online only, boost-free?? car. The Nakamura Rai Jin!

Here are some pics from the forums of PS3 Elites already using it:

Nakamura Rai Jin

It probably will be available on loan to everybody in a calender event, but for now only 64 have been shipped for keeps, to the Criterion team, a select group of press and the Elite forum posters.

Unfortunately for me and the other Elite poster on 360 our cars have had a slight manufacturing fault on the production line, so we wont be driving them until that has been sorted... not sure when my car will be delivered yet!

Criterion Games have said that they will be rewarding players who contribute to the community and take part in calender events or people who are just great at the game. So keep playing and you too could be the owner of an exclusively cool car!
