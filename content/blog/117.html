It’s amazing what an itchy nose or eye can do to the outcome of a race in Burnout. In the blink of an eye everything can be lost! Just one little scratch and you’ve lost the race that you were sure to win. It seems like the spring is the season for stupid crashes for me. But don’t think that everything is really lost… I have a secret weapon for you. Just start talking about you itchiness to the other racers and with a little luck you will see them crash within seconds 😃.
Try it next time you race and see what happens. Happy racing!
