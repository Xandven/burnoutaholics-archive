 I came up with this alternative to the weekly Wednesday Burnoutaholics night.  People like me that are in school are busy on Wednesday with homework and such, and Friday is the day where we rush home just so we can relax and do whatever.

If you want to be a part of a Weekly Friday thing, please post here.  I want this idea to take off just like the Weekly Wednesday thing.  

I am not able to make it online on Wednesdays when I have school, so Friday sounds like the next best thing to me.  I'm assuming that I'm not the only one here in this sort of situation...
