Since there needs to be a schedule to get people on Burnout 3 at the same time to race, here it is:

MONDAYS to THURSDAYS - 9pm UK (4pm EST, 10pm EU) & 2am UK (9pm EST, 3am EU)

 

FRIDAYS - 10pm UK (5pm EST, 11pm EU)

& 3am UK (10pm EST, 4am EU)

 

SATURDAYS & SUNDAYS - 6pm UK (1pm EST, 7pm EU) & 11pm UK (6pm EST, 12am EU) & 2am UK (9pm EST, 3am EU)


 

Additional notes:

These times are gathering times. I suggest if you want to play B3 get online and sit in the US Central (top) lobby a couple of minutes before the hour mark but this goes for ANYTIME not just the stated suggestions.

If your room has space for more players just run 2 lap races/road rages so that anyone waiting in the lobby will see your game more quickly.

Post replies here to highlight specific days/times upcoming that you intend to play. Ideally you could post the at least the night before to give others time to see it. If you check this post daily or the copy I put on the Burnout 3 xbox live forum then we should get some good rooms going.
