Better say hi now after having lurked here a while...

Who am I? Girl from northern Denmark in my thirties (and don't ask how high), who loves to relax with some Burnout. I have Takedown, Revenge (original and 360) and of course Paradise.

I kinda miss the traffic checks from Revenge, since I now have to get better at the skilled driving (booooring!). Was way fun to smash the other drivers off the road. And it was quite therapeutic after having driven in a real car in a traffic jam. Oh well... I'll just have to get better and at least *try* to stop saying so many bad words about the traffic on the roads in Paradise City.

I've never done Live gaming, but am actually considering it with Paradise. I'm just not sure about it... and time is a bit limited (am the sole owner of a company and can be quite busy at times). One of my favorite things about Burnout is though to sit down with burner friends and revert to early teens while driving like maniacs. Great fun and an excellent way to spend too many hours.

Anyway, time to get going, lurk some more and see if I can find some time to play around soon... been over a week since the xbox was last on.
