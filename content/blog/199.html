EA announced on the 1st August 2007 that the servers for Burnout Revenge on the PS2 and Xbox will be shut down on the 1st of November 2007.

This announcement was publicised on a few forums but nobody seems very concerned, so I suppose EA have made the right decision. 

I was not surprised by the announcement as the Xbox version of Revenge was like a ghost town online pretty much as soon as the 360 version was released.

But, I thought the PS2 version may have been more popular as this is the only version of Revenge available on Playstation.  Where is the PS Burnout community?

The Burnout 3: Takedown servers are still active on all formats and it continues to be a popular online game. Lets hope it stays popular, it would be a tragedy if the Takedown servers were closed. I haven't played Burnout 3 online for a while tho. I will have to start playing again to do my bit to maintain its popularity.

I'm joining the Burnout 3 Revolution!
