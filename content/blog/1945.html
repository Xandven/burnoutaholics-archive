As BurnoutAholics.com maintenance was long overdue I spent a couple of hours doing some changes to the site today.

Lots of the things I have changed most of you probably will not even notice.

Changes that affect everyone:

  • Flickr stream has been removed from the left margin.
  • Syndicate has been renamed "Subscribe to RSS" and moved to the top of the left margin.
  • Minor update to "Terms of use". Consequences of posting spam are now stated in the "Terms of Use".
  • Updated / removed dead links:
    •  Criterion Games Bugzilla => http://bugzilla.criteriongames.com/burnout => Updated
    • 360Voice.com => http://360voice.gamerdna.com => Updated
    • http://www.racersparadise.co.uk/ => DEAD => Removed
    • http://gamerscoreblog.com => DEAD => Removed
    • http://xboxlivelink.com => DEAD => Removed
    • http://www.burnoutteamrocks.com => DEAD => Removed

Changes to the experience of Burnoutaholics.com for anonymous users:

  • Comments are no longer accessible to anonymous users.
  • Unregistered users can no longer vote on polls.
  • Submitting feedback requires user to solve a captcha.
  • Google AdSense added to footer and right margin

Most of the above changes have been done to discourage spammers. 

As there is not much going on in the World of Burnout at the moment this site is pretty much dormant. There is hardly any activity at all and number of unique hits have fallen to around 200 a day which is not much compared to the around 2000 unique daily visitors we had back in the day. It might interest you to know that since we opened our doors back in 2007 we have had over 1700000 page views and over half a million unique visitors.

Once any news about the next Burnout title is revealed we will be back with a bang. Until then take care and have fun.
