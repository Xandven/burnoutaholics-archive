With paid DLC the developers have the opportunity to add up to 250 gamerpoints worth of achievements and upto 99 different new achievements, at least Orange Box added 99 so presumably anybody else can add that many.

CG added 4 new trophies with the Legendary car pack for PS3, here is a list of the trophies:

Win a Stunt Run in the Jansen 88 Special.
Light up the Manhattan Spirit when online in a Freeburn Challenge.
Sound the Cavalry Bootlegger's horn whilst jumping and then successfully land any Silver Lake Super Jump.
Score a 3x Boost Chain in the GT Nighthawk.

They also added 8 trophies for the Party Pack and there will be 2 more with the Boost specials and 3 more with the Toy cars... making a total of 17 new Trophies so far, where the Xbox has only had 2 new Party Pack achievements.

I really don't understand why CG didn't put in the same achievements for Xbox 360 as the trophies added for PS3, surely they aren't planning on putting 95+ achievements in the Island and Cops and Robbers packs??

Do you agree with me that CG should have added achievements with the DLC cars? Or do you think Gamerscore really doesn't matter?

I would much rather see lots of 5g achievements then just a couple at 25/50/100g.
