First good bike shop that helped me as a kid.... Didn’t give it much thought when could have yesterday , i’ll try today. All the experience of the bike game has been comparable to playing a typical Road Rash game without the Racing; you start out with a big black Ratty bike, heavy and slow is your entitlement thus far, to begin @ the begwin. Have to repair them by going to dodgy breakers who rip you a new one sometimes but your eyes are open? Lol Soon somehow, if it’s in your blood, interest is picqued enough to move forward, upgrade, gain a bit of respect, then new avenues open and now there’s this grapevine thing and you want in one way or another... Better Breakers out there, even top class Dealers that are Pro’s, again who knew really but if it’s a game, insert a coin lol
 
Was going for some theory test somewhere in the next town over and happened across a bike shop not far from this test centre, on a one way system so had to circle round to get back to it; any excuse to ride a bike further is, well, you just do it.
 
Parked up this little TZR125 sports bike outside (I was 16/17 and not very legal to ride but close enough)! Asked the biker/hippie geezer behind the counter if he’d mind me leaving it outside his shop for half an hour and he was cool. That was Steve G, the other Steve I would meet real soon. Weeks later I was scooting about and had a total revelation, I’d been bump starting this 125 for months and it never occured to me to look at why, I hadn’t been bothered, that’s completely stupid I know, it’s true either way haha Anyway it turned out the engine had a spline sticking out for a feckin kickstart... I had thought it was just an out and out sports bike, like on the grid when people push the racers from behind to start their bikes? Kin ‘ell. 
I set out to see (can make a long story short here, don’t worry) Steve, the longhaired beardy guy who helped me with the parking but met the Other Steve, like Ogri’s build but atypical “Good” Hells Angel, this time. Was well shy but had the right questions and it was a fast encounter, it won’t be forgotton. Yes he had a used kickstart, a fiver; “would you fit it for me”?.... and that was it, the kickstart was on and I was gone for a fiver. Fast and right back, ya got me.
 
It was a place to get oil or your 1st ever set of brake pads for your bike; it was a breakers and it was dirty and dusty under the Red Velvet sheet that hung half over the counter, too much Goth and funny now but it was real, you could touch it all. I’d overhear other bikers asking Steve(s) for advice and was then in my head,“Does this mean I don’t have to figure all this shit out myself..... there are people who advise as part of their jobs”?!  Cool. Sorry, for this, I’m reminiscing and I shouldnt be trying to put the now into when but skip to the end or switch?
 
Went to a main dealers on Steve(s) recommendations for a job, another good shop, in fact a great place where things ran above board, nothing underhand or dodgy and well established customer bases, Doctors and Pilots, Car Dealers Priciples, staff family members and ants crawing up my hairy legs lol  I became one of Steve(s) parts supplier as they went bigger and it was strange ethereal advising them, my oldest wiser friends who treated me like an equal when I was a kid in this unknown growing circle, Loved it and no it’s not a job anymore, they are all places to be and call me sad if wish. Almost like giving up your right to vote, to not care who is in charge,  that feeling that it really doesn’t matter who is and I won’t complain... Didn’t want to set the world alight, sure ain’t gonna so I’ll sit here baking in my bike gear with that Beer and smoke , feeling that tomorrow is a touch away and it won’t matter if it’s Sunday or Wednesday only that it will come... hopefully.  Off to eat, but know, and maybe this is my point – if I have one - that if I can treat every kid in my shop like I was treated at the start and help mould them, like a friend and have a laugh.....?
