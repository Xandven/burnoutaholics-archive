When you sign up to BurnoutAholics.com you agree that you will not:

  • Defame, abuse, harass, stalk, threaten or otherwise violate the legal rights (such as rights of privacy and publicity) of others.
  • Publish, post, upload, distribute or disseminate any inappropriate, profane, defamatory, infringing, obscene, indecent or unlawful topic, name, material or information.
  • Publish, post, upload, distribute or disseminate any topic, name, material or information that incites discrimination, hate or violence towards one person or a group because of their belonging to a race, a religion or a nation, or that insults the victims of crimes against humanity by contesting the existence of those crimes.
  • Advertise or offer to sell or buy any goods or services for any business purpose, unless BurnoutAholics.com specifically allows such messages.
  • Conduct or forward surveys, contests, pyramid schemes or chain letters.
  • Restrict or inhibit any other user from using and enjoying the BurnoutAholics.com.
  • Harvest or otherwise collect information about others, including e-mail addresses, without their consent.
  • You are only allowed to have one BurnoutAholics.com account.

BurnoutAholics.com does not tolerate spam. Posting spam in comments or blogs will result in termination of your account. There will be no warnings. Your account will be deleted and your IP-address will be banned.

BurnoutAholics.com does not have a swear filter, however we trust you not to use innapropriate language as this is a family friendly site.

Keeping to the following guide lines should help to keep the site looking pretty.

Comments

  • Give your comment a Title. Do not use just a . or part of the body of the comment as this looks like you are repeating yourself. Do not use the same title for every comment posted.
  • Remember that the Title is shown on the Recent Comments list so keep it interesting and relevant. If you can't think of a title call it RE: "the name of the blog/story".
  • Do not write single word or very short sentence comments, if you don't have anything of substance to add think twice before posting.
  • Do not Spam or start / take part in flame wars - BurnoutAholics takes this very seriously and you risk being banned if you abuse the site.

Blogs

  • Your Blog will be read by other BurnoutAholics so try to keep it interesting or entertaining for all.
  • If you want to spell check you can copy and paste into another program ie wordpad and spell check there. Also the google bar has a spell check or you can use iespell. There is more advice about this in the forums.
  • Do not navigate or use the back button unless you have saved what you are writing or it will be lost.

Forum

  • You voted for a forum so use it! If you have a topic of discussion the forum is the place for it.
  • Any site suggestions including Poll topics should be posted in the forums.
  • Do not Spam or start / take part in flame wars - BurnoutAholics takes this very seriously and you risk being banned if you abuse the site.
