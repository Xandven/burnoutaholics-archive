I got to talk to EA tech support last night as umo had sent me a message on Sunday about the Burnout 3 servers being down and Suffur noticed it the other day too. I got home from work yesterday and tried it for myself, and got the message I expected to get:

The game can not be played online at this time, please try later.

OK - on the phone to EA tech support. The dude on the other end of the phone tells me its Tuesday, they do maintenance on a Tuesday. Try it again later.

So, I wait an hour and ring back. The next dude I speak to has a less convincing American accent and is obviously not a gamer and has no clue what I am going on about. So, I ask him where he is based. He is in the Phillipines!!!

OK.. When did that happen? I swear when I used to talk to EA tech support about the Burnout 3 servers they were much more helpful and more knowledgeable about gaming, and I'm sure they were in the UK??

Anyway.. he kindly explains to me the troubleshooting process (which I already know) to change the date on my Xbox to 2001, reset my router and modem, even unplug my Xbox for a while, cos thats got to help with my online connection issues??? 😕 

Why do so many companies outsource their contact centres to the far east and give the operators American names and accents, but not the knowledge they need to answer the calls! 😠 At least they both asked me to "Have a nice day!" which made me feel warm and fuzzy inside. 😏

I will check the servers again when I get home tonight, and if it's still not working I will be back on the phone... the saga continues...
