MANHUNT

This is a game I made up while playing the demo with some friends. It's very fun and it's a great way to pass time while waiting for more players to join the game. Below are the rules for this game. Read the rules, host some friends and prove who is the best Burner in Paradise!

 

MANHUNT RULES 

 1) A single player is selected to be "IT" by the host, or who suggested playing the game.

2) player who is it must proceed to nearest auto repair.(If he needs to swap cars he may swap first) Once he has gone through he has 20 seconds to run before other players can attempt to take him down. (stay 1-2 blocks away until his time is up)

3) If player who is it is taken down, the one who took him down is now it. He gets the same safe period as the first player. (See rule 2)

4) If player who is it remains it for more than 10-15 minutes (Hosts call) he must pass off to another player.

5) If player who is it crashes himself 20 times he must pass off. (Avoids exploitation of self wrecking before being taken down.)

6) players who go on the highway must get off after two offramps. (Less bias to faster cars)

7) If the player who is it gets taken down in a way he thinks is unfair he can call a Duel.

DUELING

When dueling the winner is it.

1) Challengers line up face to face at opposite ends of runway in easternshore airfield.

2) On ref's go the go head on and jump off the ramps toward each other. (Ref is host unless host is dueling then the host selects another player to ref for the duel.)

3) If noone is taken down from first head on then the two duelers continue trying to wreck each other inside of airfeild.

4) If the player who is already it wins, he gets 5 more minutes before having to pass it off.

5) After duel is over the winner is set to it and it goes back to normal rules.

 

 

Rules may change as we fine tune Manhunt.
