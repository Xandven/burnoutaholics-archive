It has been almost 3 years since the last major upgrade of the site. As we are expecting new members and more traffic with a new Burnout game coming out we are planning to do a major update to the site. First of all we will upgrade the site to Drupal version 6.22. This is not the latest version but at the time this is as high as we can go. Version 6.22 has a bunch of security related fixes and new features compared to the version we are running today. We are also planning to change the visual aspect of the site... possibly with a new color scheme and a new layout.

The upgrade will probably happen soon and you will see a maintenace notice when the wheels are in motion. I expect some features to be unavailable for a couple of days after the upgrade but hopefully the most important things will work straight away.
