Criterion Games announces that its latest and most ambitious downloadable update for Burnout Paradise software is now live on Playstation 3.

To access the update, simply switch on your Playstation 3 with the Burnout Paradise disk in it and accept the update to 1.30 instruction.

This is the first major new content in the "Year of Paradise". The next major update, codenamed Davis, will be available around the end of August and will bring a Burnout first - motorcycles! Plus day / night gameplay, weather and much, much more.

360 XBox owners will have to wait another 14 days for the Cagney update as it did not pass the tests at Microsoft.
