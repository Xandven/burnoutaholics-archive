I was thinking of calling this blog 360 v PS3, but it's not about a console war comparison thing, so or with a ? seems more appropriate.

What I want to talk about is the Burnout Community, is there a difference between the 360 and PS3? What is the difference? and should I be considering getting a PS3 to join a more active Burnout Paradise community??? 😲

In the official Criterion Games forums I am the only Elite Poster who plays on 360. The Xbox Paradise forum is pretty dead. All of the Criterion Games Live Plays would be on PS3 if 360 users hadn't kicked up a stink about it. The front page at CriterionGames.com often runs mugshots of game sessions on PS3. I don't think anyone at Criterion plays regularly on 360.

So, is there a difference in the communities? Do any of you have both systems? which do you use to play Paradise online? is there a difference? what is it? and which is "better"???

One thing about online gaming that keeps you coming back is a feeling that if you don't play you are missing out on something.

Most of the folks who seem to LOVE Paradise are on PS3, so am I missing out on something? What am I missing out on?

Are many, many more people playing Paradise on PS3?? And do they only know Burnout from Paradise? or are they veterans of the series?

With Revenge a lot of PS2 players jumped to 360 to play online, have most Burners now jumped to PS3?
