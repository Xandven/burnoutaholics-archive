As the number of visitors to BurnoutAholics.com has dropped to less than 1% of what has been the average for the last 2 years we are now shutting down the site. We know this will upset lots of people but as hardly anyone is visiting anymore there is simply no point in keeping it running. 

We have decided this should coincide with the close down of Burnout Revenge servers.

A official good bye party will be held on 12-04-2012 and at 20:00 GTM+1 the next day the doors will close for good.

We will archive the site so the content is not lost. The domain name will be sold. As it is a A+ domain name the bidding will start at $100000. 

We want to give a special thanks to SUFFUR, PsychedelicBabe, Jinno Angelo, BootlegGiant, oO EezO Oo, Xedec, ichienkai, hn6, jwillisjr, drunknnumpty, Rofer, Nero Angelo, FrozenVapor, Jasman and the rest of the community. You are all Burnout Legends! Thanks for supporting us over the last 6 years!

EDIT: As you might have noticed it is April 1st today. We are not shutting down, we will never surrender! 🙂
