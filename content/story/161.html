Criterion Games will have a hands-on ONLINE enabled playable demo of Burnout Paradise at both the Sony conference tomorrow morning and at the main "show floor" in Barker Hangar. 

This will be the first real public outing of Burnout Paradise and Alex Ward says that they are very excited to show the game for the first time. 

The demo will be on the PS3, online enabled with Playstation Eye plugged in.
