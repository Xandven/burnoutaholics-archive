 I dont know if anyone has bothered to look into it, but recently, there has been some speculation about planes coming to Paradise. Now, I know that they are not really going to add them, but even thinking about adding that type of vehicle to a car game, in my opinion, was a bad idea, because, in my opinion, it ruins the concept of Burnout. To add other vehicles that arent cars to a car game that has made 5 Burnouts only with cars, messes up what Burnout stands for. Same goes with Bikes for me. Toy cars........................................they're ok.
