UK Burner's can now get an Extra car by pre-ordering Burnout Paradise at GAME, just like the US can by pre-ordering at GameStop.

 

The Walmart Racer can be unlocked by entering "WALMART" in "Under The Hood" in the main menu of Burnout Paradise.  Which makes me wonder what other words can be entered to unlock other cars?
