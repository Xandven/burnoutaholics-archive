Total Burnout moment -

Just picked up a copy of EA's new game 'skate.' And I'm having a blast with it.  This is a really really fun game after you get the controls worked out and can start nailing the grinds.  I'm cruisin around this city just goofin around, pullin off reverse fakey double nollie kickflips into front side 50-50 grinds and kickflipping out into 360 reverse grabs to manual.  Ok maybe I made that up, but its still fun as hell.  Even crashing is a blast and some of them really do look painful.  Eventually I started trying to see how many bones I could break in a single crash which had both me and my roommate and my cat laughing our asses off.   Ok, time to go for some cool tricks again.  So I find a good spot to go for a big jump, set down a session marker and off I go, building up speed, off the ramp, smash.  Dangit, try this again. Still no dice, I need more speed so I do what anyone else would do.  Oncoming traffic right?  doesn't work so well on a skateboard but oh well, keep trying it.  My friend had to point out to me that it wasn't really helping.  skate doesn't give you boost for doing that. "BurnoutAholic moment" I thought something was up.  Why isn't my boost bar filling up? shouldn't I be going faster?  I wasn't even thinking about it.  First thing I would do after crashing is skate into oncoming honestly thinking in the back of my head that I would get up to speed faster.  Yup, BurnoutAholic.
