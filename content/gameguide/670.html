BurnoutAholics Burnout Paradise Super Jump Guide

This guide covers all of the 50 Super Jumps in Burnout Paradise. Screen shots of the all the jumps will be added shortly.

Each Super Jump is indicated by a green dot. There are 10 Super Jumps in each of the 5 regions of the city.

Palm Bay Heights: 10
Downtown Paradise: 10
Harbour Town: 10
White Mountain: 10
Silver Lake: 10

Scroll the map to find the jump you are looking for. You can click the green dots to view a screen shot of the jump.

The Super Jump, Smash and Billboard Guide maps and screenshots is created and maintained by evild. For latest version go to: http://picasaweb.google.com/evild70.
