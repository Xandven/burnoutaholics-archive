For the last week I have been in touch with EA as the Burnout 3 servers have been unavailable.

The news from EA is that the servers are closed. They have been sunsetted to make space for newer titles.

Burnout 3 is due to be available for download on Xbox 360 as an Xbox Original from the 4th of December.  Before you pay 1200 MS points be warned - You will not be able to play Burnout 3 online.

Burnout 3 is one of the best online racing games, and to close the servers just a week or two before the game is made available for download has to be the worst decision ever!
