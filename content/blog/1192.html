  • Added "New forum topics" to the right side bar
  • Moved Search, Syndicate and Subscribe from the right to the left side bar
  • If you are a PSN member (and don't have a XBL gamertag) your user profile will now show your PSN portable ID or gamercard.
  • Menus are totally redesigned. Don't think I need to go in to the details
  • Operation linked to your user profile (e.g. My Account or Private Messages) has been moved to the top of the page

I think that was it... might have done some other stuff as well but I will leave that for you to discover for yourselves.

Hope you like the changes.
