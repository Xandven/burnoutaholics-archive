I started taking driving lessons again a few weeks ago. Its been 15 years since I first got my provisional licence and although I did about half a dozen lessons about 7 years ago I haven't driven since so I am starting from scratch.

I am learning in a Peugeot 207 and Xandu made my learner driver car in Forza.

I have been taking my lessons on Saturday evenings, and as its getting dark fairly early I have needed to use my headlights. The first week I was really nervous and gripped the steering wheel far too tight! When I first got going I had to ask my instructor how fast I was going because it felt really fast and I was too scared to look. . . it was 20mph!

The 2nd lesson was a lot more relaxed and I really enjoyed it, the 3rd was a bit more stressful as I had a lot to contend with. I had to drive along my main road 3 times and it being Margate on a Saturday night it was BUSY! I had to deal with kids on their bikes jumping in and out of the road, overtaking a bus, lots of zebra crossings and traffic lights, a roundabout and on top of all that it was raining! Scary!

I am really enjoying it and am looking forward to going out for a practice drive with Xandu in my mini metro! 😃
