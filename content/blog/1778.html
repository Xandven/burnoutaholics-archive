It seems like the Xbox LIVE Community Developer Program (XCDP) is again taking onboard new developers. We have been trying to join this program for over 2 years now and today I sent our third application to join the program. I don't have any big hopes of actually being accepted as it seems like this is a club for the big boys like 360voice and gamertagpics.

If I get accepted into the program I have some pretty amazing ideas that will totally change the way people think about and use BurnoutAholics.com. We are 100% commited to the community and by getting access to the xml feeds available through XCDP we will be able to integrate the gaming experience with BurnoutAholics.com in a all new way.

If we get accepted into the program XCDP will let us know within 2 weeks. I will keep you posted about what happens.
