Do you ever feel pressured to do good or win when your racing in a certain tag? Im just wondering because it seems like people expect a lot out of you when you come in online. They have certain expectation of you already.. Like when I come in online.. "Hey Nero!".. "What happened to your rank Nero?" or.. "Nero is quick man".. so naturally your pressured to win... ALL THE TIME.. So naturally, you cant step back a bit and just race for the hell of it... its I have to win everytime...because thats what they expect from you... Otherwise when you start falling back its like.. "Are you going for lap times?"
