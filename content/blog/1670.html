After playing the Island for a bit longer yesterday, it feels more than just an added area of Burnout Paradise, it feels more like Paradise 1.5.

So, what direction will CG take with Paradise 2? (if there is going to be a Paradise 2) What would you like to see? What bits of Burnout Paradise should they keep? What should they expand on? What should they scrap??

I would like to see racing in Paradise 2 in the same style as Island racing, with visual checkpoints which aren't on junctions. With a better race editor, possibly where you drive around and click where you want the checkpoints to be.

I would like to see a new city designed more like the Island expanding on Stunt Runs and crazy stunts as I think this is really where Burnout Paradise has found it's niche.

I would like to see online Road Rage tweaked for an open world, it just doesn't work the way it is in Paradise, I think CG need to go back to the drawing board on that one.

More competitive gameplay is needed tho, to really keep players coming back for more. Whether this is to beat other players in races, or timed challenges or just to improve on your own times. I think Paradise needs more of a competitive edge to really give it long term appeal. Although, I'm sure we will still be freeburning for some time to come!

I think the game has come a long way in the last year and a half, and if the direction of the Island is an indication of what a sequel might be like, the future for Burnout Paradise is looking a lot brighter then it was last week.
