A Man's Resolve…..                    
                       
Another customer story so skip if you like - if your still with me of course lol Few years back guy called Pete buys a Triumph from us 
and we don’t see much of him afterwards but he's a cool guy who likes to dress slightly Indian so I'm guessing he's  
travelled quite a bit too - out of the blue he's back on the phone to me 2 years back ordering  silly       
little nuts and bolts and fixture brackets for his cowls - and that's majorly boring but I treat him well and get on with things 
and I'm noticing he always sends somebody else to collect his parts or he has them posted to him……  so I'm guessing he's 
a busy man!  Couple of month back from now Pete calls in - on crutches walking very carefully. "You a'right Pete? Not Binned your 
Truimph have you mate"? Understatement if ever there was one.              
                       
He tells me he was riding along, 'bout 60mph in a 40mph (bit naughty) and he either sneezes or is blinded by the sun momentarily,
the car in front had come to a bit of a grindin' halt and he was seconds away from colliding with it so he threw his bike to the right,
glancing blow off the right rear of this car and he's sent directly into oncoming traffic at some speed - into a head on with another 
car….  Smashed over the bonnet and once prone on the road a large van runs over his midrift - both front and rear wheels.  
What a nightmare - it happened in seconds - how is this man alive?            
                       
I've never asked anyone if it's possible to mend a broken spine but if his words are true then it simply must be.  Now Pete can't feel his
feet anymore - he WAS walking very carefully indeed with two crutches but what determination from a person!  All he wanted to do
was to recover and ride again and thus nuts and bolts and fixtures off me yet he never once mentioned what had happened to him
until he saw me face to face - I couldn't believe it - his story, his recovery, his resolve to repair and modify the same  
bike he smashed himself to bits on.  All the controls you use with your feet have been moved to his handlebars where all the 
rest of the controls are, amazing I think.  Humbled in your presence dude I was!
