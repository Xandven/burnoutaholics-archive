Just over a week ago Criterion Games tweeted that they were selling a signed & framed Burnout 3 poster on Ebay, the starting bid was £99 with all proceeds going to charity (not sure which one, I will have to find out).

I was a bit hesitant about bidding at first as it is quite a lot of money, but when I saw that somebody else had placed a bid my impulse to buy it to add to our Burnout collection was overwhelming, so bid I did. I started at £101 as a Burnout significant number, as this was outbid by the first bidder's highest bid, I went up to the next Burnout significant number of £160! YIKES!! I was with Xandu and Suffur at the time.... I think they both think I am slightly mad... I was the highest bidder at £103 now all I had to do was wait til the next morning to see if I would win!

Thankfully nobody else bid (I guess nobody else is as crazy as me!) and I won the auction. I sent an email to ask about shipping, and will get it shipped to Norway to add to our Burnout exhibit at BurnoutAholics HQ now also known as the Burnout Museum!
