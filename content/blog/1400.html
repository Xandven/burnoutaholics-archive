For those of you who are PS3 users take a look at your BP trophies. In addition to the previously added trophies for the Party Pack trophies are now showing for the new cars. Trophies are listed for the legendary cars and the boost specials as well as the toy cars.

Trophies are:

Win a Stunt Run in the P12 88 Special
Light up the Manhattan Spirit during an Online Challenge
Score a Boost Chain of x3 in the GT Nighthawk
Sound the horn on Silver Lake Super Jump in the Cavalry Bootlegger
Survive a Marked Man in the Extreme Hot Rod (I think this was offline)
Complete a Timed Challenge using all 3 Boost types in the Hawker Mech
Win a Race in a Toy Car
Barrel Roll a Toy Car in the Airfield
Complete a Bike Timed Challenge using the Toy Bike
 

Of particular interest is the trophy for the Hawker Mech which requires the use of all three boost types to complete a timed challenge. Sounds like selectable boost types on the car.

To see the trophies yourself go to the compare trophies function on the XMB. When the list of games appears scroll down to BP and select - the trophy comparison should show the new trophies at the bottom.
