Xbox 360 owners may have noticed an advert on the dashboard for a game called Cobra 11 Crash Time. I noticed it and got curious so I went searching for more information.

There are some trailers and game footage on YouTube Here

There are screen shots and an interview with the Project Manager Here

You can also download a demo for PC Here

We tried the demo out and it is kinda like pursuit mode from Burnout 2, we had to chase down and stop a speeding car in an unmarked police car.

The 360 game is offline only, but is also only £29.99 on release, worth checking out for old school Burners I think! 🙂 If we do pick it up, we will get a mini review of it up on the site soon!
