 Hello, again. I've accomplished that using different cars, and getting used to different cars in Paradise and virtually every Burnout game, is good for the Burner in you. Practicing with different cars on Burnout helps, because you are eventually going to have a wide variety of vehicle knowledge and skill. This is great, because if you are in an online match, and the host decides to start a race with " Host's Choice " on, you will be prepared to drive any car because you would have practiced already with that car, and you would have known how it handles, how it turns, it's sensitivety, what speeds you will be able to reach with it, if you can take someone down to your advantage, how to take someone down to your advantage, and more. I personally have already tried doing this on Paradise, and let me tell, it was a great success. And that example that I gave you is not the only thing you will benefit from doing this. You will also be that much more of an awesome Burner!
