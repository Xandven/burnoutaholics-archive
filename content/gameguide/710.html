Paradise City has 11 parking garages. All of the parking garages are located in Palm Bay Heights, Downtown Paradise and Harbour Town. There are no parking garages in Silver Lake and White Mountain. Below we have created a map that will help you find any of the 11 parking garages. Most people will probably find them by exploring the city but if anyone is having trouble locating a parking garage the below map can be of help.

When you have found all 11 parking garages you will be awarded the "Paid and Displayed" achievement for 20 gamerpoints.
