Surprise! We are still here.

Burnoutaholics.com has been around for over 15 years and is the last one standing of all the original Burnout fan sites.

Most of the content has been preserved and we have recently moved the site to a new platform. The goal is the keep the site alive and ensure that all the content is kept for posterity.
