So what would I like the next Burnout Title to be like?

Well, In a prefect world it would be like Burnout Revenge with new tracks and cars. It would have the takedown physics from Burnout 3: Takedown and racing physics from Burnout Revenge. Of course all tracks from Burnout 2: Point of Impact, Burnout 3: Takedown and Burnout Revenge would also be included and available for online play. The same goes for the crash junctions. Lobbies like Burnout Revenge with 8 racers instead of 6 and the text-chat function from original Burnout Revenge would have been resurrected. Also the ability to name the lobbies like in Burnout 3: Takedown should be brought back. DLC Containing new tracks or game modes (not CARS!!!) would be brought out regularly (at least 4 a year for the first 3 years).

That is my perfect Burnout. What is yours?
