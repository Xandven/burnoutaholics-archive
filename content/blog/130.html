The game is great fun, like Burnout 3 Takedown, Crash and Burn, Forza, Dirt and a bit of Mario Party thrown in, will keep me going till Burnout Paradise is out(DEC07). Worth getting, funny and easy to pick up if you have played any driving game, a great fix, over Revenge, also i have a Number 1 time on the figure of 8 course, will not last long i think i can knock 2 seconds of me time, so someone will beat me. Well worth £27, though to you £35. 8 players on-line, mini games and cars blow up to 40 pieces, with flames and concussion,  only 40 less than BO Paradise. Do pick a decent car though, the first one is ok, but others are better and mod up, then buy your race car and street car.This game does stand up to Revenge, not super shiny, as i said, looks like BO3. And you can drive bonus cars, Flatmobile, yellow bus, rocket car and others. Deep Joy. Buy it you will not regret it.
