Hokay, my first Paradise post. Lets see here. Oh yes, Multistory night!

The story goes, last night I was frustrated seeing folks playing Paradise when I haven't got it so I booted up the demo with some friends, sat through the video for about the 60th time (I made a drink) and got stuck in to what soon became epic.

MULTISTORY RACING

There are three rules here.

  • Pick one Multistory Car park in the city
  • All combatants....err, racers must exit via a specified roof jump.
  • The winner lands first.

On your marks - GO.

Dont be fooled - there are no spectacular crashes here, no 400 yards barrel rolls. This is a mad dash in enclosed space. You will be shunted gently into a wall, you will be nudged off the ramp and you will verbally abuse your buddies. Its hilarious - try it!

Memorable moments?

The only one that springs to mind is failing at life, falling miles behind but reeking vengence as I landed on my competitors already battered cars to cross the finish - grins were had!

If you want to mix it up, try chaining car parks together :D
