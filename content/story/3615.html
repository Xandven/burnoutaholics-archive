Burnout Revenge Servers are still online! If you have problems connecting to the EA servers and use your gamertag on both Xbox 360 and Xbox One the solution is as follows.

1. Turn on and sign in on both consoles (Xbox 360 and Xbox One)
2. Launch Burnout Revenge on the Xbox 360 and find a race or host your own.

Returning to the lobby after a set of races seems to be pretty slow, but it works so be patient.
