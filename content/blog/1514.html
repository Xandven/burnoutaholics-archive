I'm one of the few here probably who's playing BP for the PC, and lately I really feel like Criterion neglects their PC-version of this game...

Seriously, all they would care about lately is promoting their car-packs to an utterly disgusting point (seriously: All of them posting news day by day only asking to "get the Legendary cars pack" and stuff slowly gets annoying and is unlike what Criterion seems to was before) and I've heard nothing about the PC-version since release anymore... It's like they'd slowly let it die what reminds me of Eden Games and their behavior with Test Drive Unlimited. This behavior was: Always update Xbox360 and PS3, but only update the PC once.

I really hope Criterion won't turn Burnout Paradise (PC) into a second Test Drive Unlimited, because I'm sure that most PC-gamers will hate this a damn lot.
