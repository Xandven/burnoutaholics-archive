You can show off your own favourite clips from Burnout Revenge on BurnoutAholics.com by sending a mail to clips@burnoutaholics.com. Include your gamertag and the name of the clip(s) you want to submit in the mail. Within a week you will receive a friends request from Xandu. Accept the friends request and we will download your clips and make them available on this page. When we have downloaded the clips we will send you a message.

If you have other cool videos from the Burnout series that you want to submit just include a link to the video in the mail and it will be added to BurnoutAholics.com.

Click the picture or the title to watch the clip.
