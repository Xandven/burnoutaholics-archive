As some of you probably know I've been playing some Forza 2 Motorsport lately. I finally completed the game last week after about 2 months. It took me a total of 58 hours to complete the game which shouldn’t be too bad since my winning percentage is 98%.
 
One of the last races I did was a classics race where you had to use either the Ford GT 40 or the Ferrari P330 (both had to be unmodified). Of course I selected the P330 since it handles a “little” bit better than the Gt 40 and also I think it looks a lot like the Euro Circuit racer. For some strange reason I had the difficulty setting on Hard which probably wasn’t a good idea. I was in the lead for 8 laps (I think it was a 9 lap race on Silverstone) and then this happens…😠
Had to restart the race and adjust the difficulty to medium. 🙂

I really enjoyed FM2 and I will now probably spend some more time racing online to get my 1 000 000 online credits for the last achievement. I will probably also do some more auctions as you can never really get enough money. Selling cars is really fun and it is incredible what people are willing to pay for a paint job. I’ve probably made about 20 000 000 on auctions so far which made it really easy to get the all cars achievement.
