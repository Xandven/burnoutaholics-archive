After a little over 20 hours of gameplay I acquired my Burnout license.  A slew of cars unlocked including a few more of the "code" cars like the Walmart racer which is awesome for setting road rules I might add!  So far I'm loving the game.  I'm a little over 50% complete and it tells me I now need 111 wins for my Burnout Elite license.  I have around 30 cars and still need quite a few discoverables as well. 

The game in general is great fun and I thought I'd mention a few things here for discussion...

1.  Traffic does seem to pop-in at times when you are going really fast and can be frustrating.

2.  I do notice a blip or stutter occasionally when going really fast, it's like a subsecond pause in the action and can be very distracting at times.

3.  Road Rules online times are going to be the most fun in my opinion to keep the playability alive on this game.

4.  Stunt runs are HARD

5.  When the burnout servers are unreachable the only way to get back into the game is to go to dashboard and reload it

6.  Marked Man runs are almost impossible once  you reach the level I'm on

Again, this game is a lot of fun and I've decided that it's my favorite burnout game yet and I've loved them all.  The open world is great fun.

Thoughts?
