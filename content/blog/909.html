I live in the arson capital of the UK, possibly the world! At least it feels that way. There are definitely a disproprotionate number of deliberate fires for such a small town!

Last Friday the latest arson to hit Margate was in the Tivoli Arcade on the sea front. There are (or were) 3 large arcades on the Margate sea front, although they are mostly slot machines now there are still a few video games to be played.

BBC News report on the fire

When I walked passed the Tivoli Arcade this morning, there were work men removing the charred remains of a few of the 2p slots along with one of my arcade favourites, House of the Dead. 🙁

Arcade Fire Photos

This isn't the first large arcade fire on the sea front, the last one was in 2003 and the buildings were destroyed!

Big Arcade Fire 2003

I love popping into the arcade on a sunny summer afternoon to lose a few coins in those colourful cabinets, a pass-time which is fading away or in this case - going up in smoke!
