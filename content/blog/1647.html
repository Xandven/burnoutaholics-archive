TotalVideoGames.com has posted a FUEL review, giving the game a measley 5/10! You can read the full review HERE.

The reviewer makes several comparisons with Burnout Paradise, mostly as Paradise delivers more to do in an open world. FUEL seems to be all about the races, but the AI aren't much of a challenge. Online however might be a different story, but in order to take full advantage of the possible 16 player online races, enough people will need to get past the dodgy reviews and buy the game and give it a go.

Unlocking cars seems similar to Paradise too, they appear in the open world and you have to go and "tag" them in order to get to drive them yourself.

I thought Paradise was a bit too much of just an Open World Environment over Great Gameplay, but it seems that FUEL has taken this a step further. A HUGE open world, but sadly that seems about it...

I guess Xandu and I might just get the 1 copy to check it out, and then only purchase a 2nd if it seems that the online is going to be something we want to play together.

As I am not a big fan of the whole open world racing thing, this may not be the game for me.

I'm not really sure now where my next racing fix will come from, perhaps I should put all my hopes on Split/Second??
