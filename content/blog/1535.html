I don't know how many people who play Burnout Paradise were alive in 1983, I imagine that for a lot of you this is way before your time and you may not get the reference. But, the new Crash TV is based on BBC 2's Open University shows and features Professor Follett teaching us all about Boost.

Crash TV episode 27: The University of Paradise

First up there is a history lesson, so for those of you whose first experience of Burnout is Burnout Paradise you get a glimpse of the first 4 games in the series. The games which for me define Burnout. Then you get to see the way Boost has been changed in Paradise, and how the Boost works in the new Boost special cars; The Hawker Mech and The Hot Rod Extreme.

Seeing the evolution of Boost and how now with the new Hot Rod you don't even have to earn boost. You can take any line you like and do not have to think about trying to get oncoming or near misses. This is just more evidence that Burnout has strayed too far away from it's roots. Burnout was once a simple game with a few key ingredients that made it stand out in the racing genre:

1) Close, fast, intense race action!

2) Boost that had to be earned through dangerous driving!

3) Crashes!

Yes, there are some of these elements in Burnout Paradise, but if you go back to play the previous Burnout's you will see how different a game it is. I really hope there is some Devolution in the next incarnation of Burnout, to bring back the 3 key points that for me, make the Burnout experience.
