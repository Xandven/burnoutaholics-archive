Burnout Paradise is the latest instalment in the Burnout game series. It was released in January 2008 on XBOX 360 and PS3. It is not just the next evolutionary step in the game series but more of a revolution. Burnout Paradise is an open world game which includes lots of new game modes like Stunt Run, Show Time, Marked Man, Challenges and Motor Bikes.

Burnout Paradise has a solid single player mode where you can compete in race,  stunt run, road rage and marked man events to gain licences and unlock cars. This all happens without loading and the entire city is unlocked for you to explore straight away.

Criterion Games has put a lot of effort into making the online game easily accessible to the player and to build a strong online community. Traditionally one would think of racing, road rage and crash as the primary online game modes. This is not the case in Paradise City. According to Criterion Games Freeburn is the most popular online activity. Freeburn includes challenges, road rules and online games like stunt run, road rage and marked man.
