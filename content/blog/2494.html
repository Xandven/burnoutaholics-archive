I've done some minor updates to the site today. The most important one is that the text editor (ckEditor) has been updated to the latest versions. For IE 10 users this means that it is no longer required to use compability view when you add blogs or comments.

I would also like to mention that we have now been spam free since November 2012! To give you some perspective on how big the spam problem really is I can reveal that in the last two years 51423 spam posts have been automatically reported and deleted by our "watch dog".  In the last couple of months we have put in place a few new measures that have made it almost impossible for spammers to target our site. We are very happy with the results!

We are about to hit a couple of milestones on the site! This month we turned six! Happy birthday to us! We are also about to hit 650K unique visitors and 2 million page views. This great! What is not so great is that the number of visitors to the site is at a all time low... which is understandable since the Burnout franchise is in hibernation.
