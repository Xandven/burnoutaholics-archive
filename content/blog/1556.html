My least favourite bit about running this website is when I feel the need to moderate.

I have added a line to our terms of use:

BurnoutAholics.com does not have a swear filter, however we trust you not to use innapropriate language as this is a family friendly site.

If you haven't read the Terms of Use in full please take a second to have a look. If recent behaviour continues I will take more serious action then unpublishing and locking threads. I haven't had to ban anyone for their behaviour yet, and I really don't want to have to start now.

Be excellent to each other, and party on dudes! 🙂

Also, sorry it took so long for us to take action on this, but we had a weekend away which was lovely!
