The complete Legendary Cars pack has now been revealed over at CriterionGames.com. In the first full Crash TV episode of 2009 there is more footage of the Jensen 88 Special and a first look at the other 3 cars in the Legendary pack.

Crash TV episode 20

The cars are going to be available both online and offline and have more under the hood then their un-legendary counterparts. They each have special features when you press L3 on the controller.
