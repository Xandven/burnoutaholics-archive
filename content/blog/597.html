I went over to www.3drealms.com today to see if they had any news about Duke Nukem.... WOW! They have actually released a teaser trailer. They've been working on this game for over 10 years now and we haven't really heard anything about it since E3 2001. 

Anyway... the trailer is out. Go to www.3drealms.com to select your download source.

 

This teaser is not brilliant and it does not show any game play but I guess it is better than nothing.

I've always been a huge Duke fan and I was really excited when DNF was first announced back in 1996... today I'm not excited at all. This is a game I've been waiting for since 1996... if it is released next year or in 2009 doesn't really matter. The only thing we know for sure is that the game will be released "When it is done"!!!
