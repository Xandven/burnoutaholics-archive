Hello and welcome!

As you may know, we have something called "winter holidays" here, at Poland. It lasts about 2 weeks, but even that is so short, we're enyoing it. To celebrate the begining of winter holiday 2008, I decided to play Burnout a bit. Okay, that's a lie actually, because I'm playing every day. Anyway, I was playing online today in japanese demo verison (I LOVE Japanese CrashFM! :D "You're tuned to CrashFM! <(Japanese Atomika speaking their language)> Make sure you stay with CrashFM!" This mix is awesome) and as usual, they were keep kicking me out. So I decided to launch european version and jump around in old good Hunter Cavalry. After playing all events (Stuntrun usual score, around 1 500 000) I asked myself "Why not try to beat my latest record? And take photos this time?". So, I did it. Layds & Gents, my latest record in Stuntrun (Brand new, I made it about 10 minutes ago) is...

4 333 640 points.

And I'm damn proud of doing that in about 7 minutes. I wasn't controlling myself. It's not conscious driving, it's like being in other world. Completely cut from reality. And perfoming stunts and drifts is not based on thinking, but on instinctive reactions. I think that I wouldn't be able to act konwingly having something like "120 000 x 34" on my screen. I can't tell you exactly how is it to play without thinking, but I know one thing - I  am extremely enjoying it. It's amazing. I think that saying "Playing Burnout is a state of mind" is something explaining it the best way. Before I forget: Off-screen pics.

Hey, Criterion, is that enough to join your "Millionaire Club"? :)
