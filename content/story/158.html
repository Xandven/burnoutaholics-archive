Today Criterion Games released a new new Burnout Paradise in game footage video. You just have to see this one! The video will open in a new window. 

The video is from the pre-alpha E3 release of Burnout Paradise. It shows some truly amazing crashes. You also get to see a couple of the crashes in slow-motion. You can see sparks, glass and metal flying as the car is crushed.  

The sense of speed in Burnout Paradise seems to be just as good as in the previous Burnout games. Paradise City looks stunning. Can’t wait try this for my self sometime in Q4 2007…

Hopefully we will get more in-game footage in the coming days.
