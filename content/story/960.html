On the 1st of May 2007 BurnoutAholics.com as we know it today opened its doors to the public. As some of you might know the site has actually been around since July 2006 but the first 10 months was used to try out various ideas and technology for the site.

To celebrate our 1 year anniversary we decided to have a birthday party. We invited some of our most active members to the BurnoutAholics HQ in Margate, UK.

BurnoutAholics Dominator representative Jasman traveled all the way from Norway to take part in the celebrations.

We had two Xbox 360s set up for the party. The most popular games were Guitar Hero II and III and Sega Super Star Tennis. Burnout Paradise was also played as we all helped to complete a few events to get SUFFUR on his way to elite.

We got a special birthday cake that looked a bit like the Revenge Racer with flames on the hood. Burnout merchandise was given to four of our most valued members to thank them for their contributions to the site.

The celebration was concluded with the BurnoutAholics Crazy Golf Tournament on Sunday afternoon. The tournament was won by drunknnumpty closely followed by Xandu and SUFFUR only 4 and 5 strokes behind. Two players managed to get a hole in one, well done!

As this event was successful and private we might do a public Meet and Eat ™ at some point in the future.
