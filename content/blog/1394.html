Yes! Me!!                                                

I am an Xbox Live Ambassador and have been for some time now. I haven't really advertised the fact so thought I would do a blog to let you all know a bit about the Xbox Ambassador programme.

Here are the details from Xbox.com:

Xbox Live® Ambassadors 

Welcome to the Xbox Live Ambassadors programme. It's a way for the newest explorers of gaming's online frontier to meet the veterans of the Xbox Live arena. Why? So they can learn the ropes, and get a smooth, friendly introduction to the most exciting revolution in gameplay around.

Whichever end of the Xbox Live experience curve you're on, Ambassadors is a great way to get involved in the community. 

New Player?
You might be feeling a little intimidated. You don't just want to get wiped out without knowing what's going on. Here's where you can find a living 'guide' who plays your game and speaks your language. One that remembers their own first time on Xbox Live - the thrill and the fear - and is willing to let you in on some of tricks of the trade. All you have to do is go to Find an Ambassador, choose one that's right for you, and ask them to play. That's where the fun (and the challenge) begins. You can send us feedback on the experience afterwards. 

Xbox Live Veteran?
You've been around the block a couple of times, so you've got a solid friends list and know how it all works. This is your chance to become a vital member of the Xbox Live community - sharing your hard-won knowledge and experience with growing legions of plugged-in gamers. With over 50% of consoles now connected, you're ready to help lead the way into a brave new world of gaming and bring a touch of class to the whole experience.

So, if you are fairly new, or completely new to Xbox Live and have any newbie questions that you are afraid to ask... or if you want to find a game which is a bit more newb friendly... seek out an Ambassador, and we will be happy to help! If you do send any friend requests out make sure you put a message with them, as they are then less likely to be deleted!

Happy Gaming!  🎮  🙂
