Last week we changed hosting (it was not my choice) as the hosting provider we have used since 2006 has shut down. This means that we have moved to a new server and database and that SSL have been enabled on the site. Most noticeable difference is that you now navigate to https://burnoutaholics.com which means modern browsers will not be complaining about insecure connections.

The new hosting costs 3x as much a year as the previous one so it is time to migrate the site to a new platform. I have not decided how or when to do this, but we have over 3600 stories and blogs that needs a new home.
