So the drift'gasm, I have used this word before many years ago, but saying in a room of BO Revenge the other day, got people asking, so I will try to explain?I think I did it better explaining in the room, but hey i blog once in while? 

Now with Revenge, its all about speed, with the bug, gaining more speed, but since i'm not that great at this boost bug, I just try to race, keep a consistant speed and not crash, hit objects etc. Now I like to drift around corners or through the track obstacles (wow spelling issuses are turning up, i need my word back and my dic' program, but i Blackl'ed it now as it did bug me so!), like White mountain etc. The object is to drift, not losing speed but drift in a consistant arc, through traffic and not touching the sides as it were. (wow dirty minds out there).

A good example of this in on White Mountain, drifting up hill through the tunnel, near-missing the traffic, gaining boost, and keeping that going up the hill. It's sort of S bends stuck together and you're dirfting it all sideway flowing left and right, yes it may be a bit slower than normal, but the drift-drive part is so much fun for me, that i feel great pleasure doing it, and before you ask it's all in the mind not some physical experience, again dirty minds, tisk-tisk.

I also get the drift'gasm going on most tracks, it's that 'everything is going right', the 'flow' is with you, you're getting away with just missing stuff, going from one corner to another, it's so smooth, you think you using a razor across your face or whatever else you may trim. Angel Valley, from the Burger Sign till the drain, you can get that boost jump from the burger sign up down, and then near miss cars off that jump, hit that right corner, near miss like a mofo, lefty, then righty through the petrol/gas station with a smooth arc that goes between the traffic hold straight, through the alley way to the drain. Lone Peak is another good drift'gasm track. Even Central route too.

Its all about the smooth continous drift with the dangerous driving, so how many times to explain, it's just about doing it all right and feeling good.
