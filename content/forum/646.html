GameSpot just demoed live the full version (a pre-release build I think they said) of Burnout Paradise on their "On the Spot" show. 

You can watch the show here...

http://www.gamespot.com/shows/on-the-spot/

They showed a brand new never before seen trailer and played the game in real time.

Man the game looks awesome! The complaint the guy playing the demo had (he only had been playing for a few hours) was that it was a bit confusing in that there were no large arrows showing you which way to go. He said if he looked at the map he often crashed. I think this (as I have discovered from playing the demo a lot ) will become a non-issue once you learn the city. I hardly ever look at the map now in the demo.

The live pre-view they did showed off some really cool areas that weren't in the demo. And I saw a lot of really kick ass jumps. They also showed how you acquire new vehicles by winning events and taking down the vehicle when you see it on the streets (which was cool.)

Man I can't wait until Jan 22nd!!   :D
