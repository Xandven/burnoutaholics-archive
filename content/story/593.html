EA has today announced the full track listing for the Burnout Paradise soundtrack, which is headlined by Guns N' Roses' rock anthem Paradise City. In total the soundtrack will include songs from forty major artists including Adam and the Ants, Depeche Mode, Jimmy Eat World, Soundgarden and The Pigeon Detectives.

The Burnout Paradise soundtrack features a truly volatile mix of rock's most dangerous tracks -- beginning with the iconic 'Paradise City' -- for an audio fuel as explosive as the game itself.

In addition to this Burnout Paradise also inluces the soundtrack from Burnout 1, Burnout 2: Point of Impact and the original soundtrack for Burnout 3: Takedown. This is the music that was replaced by the EA-TRAX soundtrack that is in the game today.
