Morning Bloggy, I had a great night  again last night but it wasnt on Revenge (will I get flamed for that comment) Halo 3 finally dropped through my letterbox and guess what, yep thats right I don't like the campaign (there said it) all I have to do now is say it on a fan-site, but the multilayer is amazing, for anyone that has played Halo 2 there was a map called Zanzibar well its back and its been tarted up a little, talking is a bit of a nightmare as you have to press the d-pad and a few people seem to have trouble in doing so, the maps are large and fun with a couple of new vehicle, Mongoose is a quad bike which is pointless to drive as you are easy sniper bait but fun all the same, on one of the outside levels you have a big truck that moves all by its self which is extremely fun if all your team are in it, (I think its a mobile base) there is also a a bike sort of thing which rocks at running people over, even to the die hard Burnout fan-boy I would recommend this game, if only to have a fun night away from your supremacy as a burnout god, I give it 10/10..
