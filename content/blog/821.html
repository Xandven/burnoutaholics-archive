Last night I decided to host some online races in Burnout Paradise. I set up a 3 round race and set the access to open. Within 10 seconds I had a full room. 7 people were all asking me to start the race. Apparently several of them had been looking for races for a while and they were all tired of doing challenges. I had a couple of people asking me to turn traffic off but I normally ignore this kind of request. The only time I see a point in having traffic off is when trying out new routes. Traffic stayed on all night!

When racing with people I don’t know I try to select races that are not too complicated at first. This means races going pretty much in a straight line like the Webster Av. Drag Race or the race going from the Baseball Stadium to the Lone Stallion Ranch. Any race going along South and North Mountain Drive also works well. I kept the races simple for the first 6 rounds and they were all close. Most of the time only one or two DNF. It was also really fun that people were using different cars. Someone even used the 4x4 and it was causing havoc to my PCPD Racer 😃.

This was the first night when I really had fun doing online races in Burnout Paradise. To me mixing challenges, road rules, today’s best and races does not really work well. If I want to race I want to race… I don’t want to go clubbing at the Country Club or Meet up at the Baseball Stadium or do a triple barrel roll through the Aircraft fuselage in the airfield. Don’t get me wrong; challenges and all the other stuff rocks, but if I’m in a racing mood that is what I want to do. Give me quick races like in Revenge or Takedown and I’m a happy boy.👍
