A BurnoutAholic is a person who is totally devoted to the burnout series developed by Criterion. He or she has played all of the games in the series and regularly plays at least one of the titles. Most BurnoutAholics play burnout a couple of hours a day. They are also generally active in the burnout community. BurnoutAholics don’t cheat or use glitches to boost their rank. Generally rank is not what drives BurnoutAholics to play the game (although some say they prefer to be in the top 1000). Currently Burnout Revenge for the Xbox 360 is the most popular game. The highly awaited sequel Burnout Paradise is due for release winter 2007.
