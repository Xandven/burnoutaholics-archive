Four more years since I last blogged on here.

I am now a Norwegian citizen.

The site has had more issues which Xandu has been working to resolve... and tada! It's resolved.

Our kids are growing up, the eldest is almost 15 and the youngest is 8.

We haven't seen much from the lads (and lasses) from Criterion/Three Fields... it all seems a little quiet on the front of anything officially happening to bring back our favourite franchise.

BUT, the community has taken things into it's own hands and Burnout 3: Takedown (amongst others) is being played on PC & PS2, ONLINE!!! With help from the use of discord servers and although I haven't played, Xandu has and I have seen the list of names and a few recognisable Burners are there.

It's kinda amazing, and a testament to the quality of the game, that people are still so interested two decades on.

The years I was playing avidly and hanging out with you all is such a special time in my life, I will always look back on it fondly!
