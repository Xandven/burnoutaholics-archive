It is fine to love this series, or even one game in the series and even to argue/debate over which is best but do we need to be constantly qualifying our statements with "true" burner/fan.

I would probably not be considered a "true" fan by some purely based on my liking Paradise, or even my preference for Crash junctions in Revenge. 

I am happy to admit that I am not the greatest fan of racing in Revenge (possibly down to sheer laziness to invest the time in learning how to boost jump consistently) but I would be crazy to suggest that it is any way a lesser experience than any that I do enjoy. That being said, I do not think that, just because you race on Revenge or B3, you are in any way more qualified to condemn those that do enjoy other aspects of the series, and especially not to pull out the "true" Burner/fan trope.

There is at least one member of this community who, by their own admission, would condemn Paradise without even having played the game, you do not have to play the game, just stop passing off secondhand information as your opinion. 

In a recent post another "true" burner cast aside the Crash junctions as irrelevant  - their dislike for Crash Junctions does not make them irrelevent [sic], any more than my dislike for Racing makes it irrelevant.
