I used to run my systems online all the time.  I have a PSWii60, all 3 consoles.  All 3 would connect with ease, gaming online was fun, fast, and easy.

All of a sudden, for no appreciable reason, none of my consoles will connect over wireless any more.  No clue why, no reason, no change.  They just stopped accepting the wireless.

Well, that is not completely true.  I can get my PS3 to the point where it talks with my router, accepts the IP, but refuses the internet.  The Wii and 360, though, do not seem to recognize any kind of wireless at all (I have the wireless adapter for the 360).

I know the signal is strong enough, as I am sitting in the same room with a laptop, on wireless, surfing the web and typing this up.  The PS3 gives me, at worse, a 95% signal strength, so I know that is not the issue.

Tomorrow, I am planning on running to the nearest computer store (about 45 minute drive) to pick up some cat-5.  Hopefully some direct connections will be able to get me back online.

I did manage to get online at a friend's house over the weekend with my PS3, and raced a bit there.  Anybody interested can add me to their friend's list, though it may be a while before I can accept you.  Hopefully, I can get the wired connection working and see everybody online sometime tomorrow.
