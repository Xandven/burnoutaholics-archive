I did some minor updates to the site today.
  • Polls page now shows all polls that has ever been on BurnoutAholics.com
  • Added "My Burnout Collection" to the user profiles. Go check out your user profile and have a look at the new stuff. You can now tell other BurnoutAholics what Burnout games you own.
  • A rating system for the blogs has now been added. I did this a couple of weeks ago but I don't think I told anyone about it 😃. If you have a look at the bottom of a blog post you will see five stars. You can rate the blog post 1 to 5 (5 is best) by clicking on the appropriate star.
Earlier today I checked out the stats for BurnoutAholics.com. Since the release of the Burnout Paradise Demo the number of unique visitors has more than doubled. The last 4 days we have had more than 300 unique visitors every day. Total number of page views since May 2007 reached a whopping 110000 today!
