Burnout Dominator is the second Burnout game available for handhelds. The game was released in spring 2007 and was developed by Electronic Arts. Burnout Dominator is only available for PSP and PS2.

The game retains the core of game play from previous Burnout games. The game introduces a new type of boost which is basically the same as boost chaining in Burnout 2: Point of Impact except that you can still use your boost even when the boost bar is not full. If you fill the boost bar all the way you get “Supercharge boost” and you chain your boost as long as you keep your boost bar filled.

Burnout Dominator includes most of the racing game modes from previous Burnout games. This includes race, road rage, eliminator and burning lap. The game also includes four brand new game modes; maniac, drift challenge, near miss challenge and burnout challenge. In maniac challenges the player drives as dangerously as possible to earn points doing drifts, air, oncoming and near misses within the given time. Chaining Burnouts in this mode increases the score multiplier.

Crash mode was not included in Burnout Dominator. The game focuses on racing events. Also the game does not include online play. The PSP version had online leader boards so you could compete for the best times with your friends. This feature does not seem to work after all Burnout game websites on EA.com was redirected to Criteriongames.com.

Burnout Dominator includes 10 tracks from 5 race locations. Two of the tracks and locations are exclusive to the PSP. Both Carnival City (South America) and Red Gate (Eastern Europe) could originally be downloaded for free from the Playstation Store. Both downloads have now been removed but luckily they can still be downloaded from various other sites.
