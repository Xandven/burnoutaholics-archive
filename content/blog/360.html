It's official:  the San Francisco Giants will not be resigning all-time home run leader and single season home run record holder, Barry Bonds, next season.

I understand that the franchise is trying to rebuild, but Bonds is a legend; an all-time great whose legacy will remembered, and not diminished or tarnished by the rumors of scandal.

Bonds may not be the player he once was in his youth, but he still is productive, and provides veteran leadership... it's disappointing to find out that he won't be returning to the Giants next season.
