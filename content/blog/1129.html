Exclusive News Brought to You by BurnoutAholics.com.... Burnout Bikes Can't Crash!*

Burnout Bike Video

Check out the video over at GameTrailers to see some footage of the bikes in Paradise and the day/night cycle. The video is a brief interview with Marketing Assistant, Kevin O'Leary where he exclusively reveals that they are "focussing more on the social aspect, playing online with your friends and doing these specific freeburn challenges, rather then the crashing."

Watch the video to see how the bikes bounce off of the walls and traffic. It doesn't look to me as though the bikes can crash at all!? 

It's another first for Burnout!

Paradise is setting new standards for the game series which started off as an arcade racer, where you drive dangerously to earn boost and you can crash your vehicle causing £££££ of insurance damage. We now have boost free cars and crash free vehicles and coming soon we will have planes!!

I do Love Paradise, and it is a lot of fun doing the challenges, and I'm sure I will enjoy doing them on Bikes too. But, it is another step away from why Burnout was made in the first place. This isn't a bad thing, there were going to be issues involved with the bikes crashing, so having them bounce is probably the easiest solution. But it is a bit of a sad thing as it will make Paradise feel even less like Burnout for me.
