It was 12 months ago today I first played PARADISE, and I loved it.
12 months on and the latest update has drastically changed the game. I'm not from Del Monte but if I was, the man from Del Monte would be shouting a resounding 'NO'. To be absolutely honest, I haven't updated myself and I can only go off the list of messages I've received so far, but the reviews aren't good. In the last 90 minutes I've been informed:
It's blue. Everything is tinted blue. Billboards are bright red but superjump markers are blue... and "blue looks sh*t". "it looks like they got their inspiration from the teletubbies"
Nearly every car is now slower. e.g. the Carbon X12 was 10 speed & 9 boost, it is now 6 speed & 9 boost.
In offline road rages you get 10 secs now per takedown until the target is reached.
Not your finest moment CRITERION, not by a long shot. I'm not going to update and I'm gonna miss that game immensley. I'm just thankful I've done all the (previous) online challenges and I hope I can buy the legendary cars from the PS Store and use them without this update... but that's doubtful. I leave you now with my favourite quote of the morning...
"OMG wot a f*ck*ng balls up Criterion!"
