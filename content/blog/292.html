My Xbox died a few days ago. I feel sorry for the little guy - those 3 red lights can't feel good. His death was slow and painful - first, he would overheat every once in a while due to the summer heat; then, he would begin to overheat even if the weather was nice and cool. A day before his death, gray vertical lines would appear across his screen after turning him on for more than a minute... and soon, the red lights of DEATH came to haunt him forever.

Hopefully, the MS repair center can cure his pain within a month.
