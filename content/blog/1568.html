The last couple of months have been extremely busy for me so I have not really had time to add much content to the site. In the last couple of weeks BurnoutAholics.com has hit a couple of big milestones. First of all we have now had over 1.000.000 page views. This is a huge number and when we started the site I had never thought we would reach this number so quickly.

As some of you may have noticed we now also have 1000 members. Thank you all for signing up!

Our Burnout Paradise Hall of Fame is getting very popular with some of our members and there are some scores on there that have been obtained in “special” ways or with special dedication. Some of the scores are obviously glitched but as long as we get the required verification the scores will still be kept on there. The rules and the way the Hall of Fame works might be changed in the near future.

I hope to be able to dedicate some more time in the coming months to update the site as I have several ideas for improvements and new features.

See you on South Mountain Drive.
