 "This is huge!", "At first I did not belive it", "Hail to the King BABY!!!!"

Today at 6pm GMT 2KGames and Gearbox announced that Duke Nukem is coming to XBox 360, PS3 and PC next year. This is amazing news and after 14 years I never thought we would hear from Duke again. I've been watching the live stream from PAX for the last hour and it seems like people are pretty excited about the news. They did not show us the official trailer due to rating issues but I guess it will be available from all relevant web sites within a couple of days. As for the game play it is hard to make up an opinion due to the quality of the stream but from what I've seen it looks pretty sweet. There seems to be everything people expect from a modern FPS; drivable vehicles, huge open environments, lots of guns, babes and monsters.

The recorded stream is here: http://www.ustream.tv/recorded/9327658 if you want to see the footage from the 2KGames booth at PAX!
