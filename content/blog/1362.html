Today Dash of Destruction was released for free on Xbox Live Arcade. Dash your way through the city as a hungry Tyrannosaurus Rex in search of a Doritos delivery truck, or as a terrified truck zooming in and out of the T-Rex’s path of destruction in search of its drop-off points.

It took me about 15 minutes to complete the game and I actually found it quite entertaining for being a free game. 😃 I especially like the fact the it was the 200 easiest gamerpoints I have ever earned. The only thing that was a little bit annoying was that I had to connect a second controller and sign in with a second gamertag to get the 10 point multiplayer achievement.

If you are a gamer score whore and want 200 easy gamerpoints this is definitely your game! 😛
