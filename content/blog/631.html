I've always wanted to do some kind of game guide for a favorite game. Playing the game and writing down what I've learned in hopes it'll help other gamers.

After playing the Burnout Paradise demo and seeing Criterion released a full map of the game I was inspired. It reminded me of a map/guide I used to find the Hidden Orbs in Crackdown. I have a computer, a video capture box and a love for the Burnout series so I got to work creating maps pointing out the locations of all Gates (smashes), Billboards and Super Jumps. You can find my work in progress at http://picasaweb.google.com/evild70/

Criterion mentioned they released the demo after the game was finished. I'm not positive the locations of the objectives we see in the demo are going to be in the game. It's a good start and I can easily change it when the full game is released. I'm looking forward to finding everything and putting on these maps for everyone. If these maps help a few people find that missing gate or billboard, I'll be happy.
