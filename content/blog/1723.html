Only problem is that this page apparently only works on IE6 (I tried IE7, IE8 and Firefox). If you are having the same problem as me the correct links are http://prince-of-persia.uk.ubi.com/epilogue/xbl/code.php for XBOX 360 and http://prince-of-persia.uk.ubi.com/epilogue/psn/code.php for PS3.
