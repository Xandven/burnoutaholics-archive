I was reading this earlier over on xbox.com and it was about a GamerchiX who has enrolled her beautiful daughter into GamerchiX. This is what the proud mother had to say...

''My daughter Keirsten is almost eleven months old. She sits in her walker next to me while I play my favorite Xbox 360 games. I gave her an old controller of mine without the battery so she can play along.''

Is Keirsten the youngest GamerchiX?

''She loves it and always has to have a controller while I'm playing. She really likes watching us play Rock Band and enjoys the language of the advisors on Sid Meier's Civilization Revolution. Here she is "playing" along with me.''

She just might be your youngest GamerchiX ever. But if you know of any little girl that will upstage this beauty then please do let me know. Im a proud member of GamerchiX and i have been for nearly 3 years now, I personally think its a great place for us Ladies to get together and get to know each other. If any of you females want to join the Xbox GamerchiX then all female gamers are invited to send an email direct to Trixie along with your gamertag to xlmail@microsoft.com with the subject: XGX.
