Applies to `content/`, `data/` and `static/files/` — the articles, news posts, forum
threads, comments, polls and images that make up the BurnoutAholics.com archive.

Code and templates are licensed separately; see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## The grant

**[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)**.

In short: you may copy, republish, adapt and build on it, including commercially, provided
you

- **give credit** to BurnoutAholics.com and link back to this archive, and
- **share alike** — distribute anything you build on the same terms.

Those two conditions are chosen deliberately. Attribution keeps the credit attached to the
work permanently, and ShareAlike means no one can take this archive, enclose it, and put it
behind a wall. Together they are the closest thing to a guarantee that the material stays
available and stays attributed.

## The limit

**Around 500 people wrote the posts and comments in this archive.**

Copyright in their words belongs to them, not to the site owner. Running a website does not
transfer copyright in what its members write, and no licence granted now can retroactively
cover contributions made by other people over nearly twenty years.

So the CC BY-SA 4.0 grant above extends only as far as the site creator's own authorship.
Everything else is published here as a community archive, under the same understanding it
was originally posted under: publicly readable on BurnoutAholics.com.

Practically, this means:

- **Reading, linking, quoting and archiving** — fine, and the point of the exercise.
- **Bulk republication of other members' posts under CC BY-SA** — not something this
  licence can authorise, because it was never the site owner's to give.
- **If you wrote something here** and want it removed or attributed differently, open an
  issue or get in touch. See below.

## Removal requests

If you posted on BurnoutAholics.com and want your contributions removed, renamed or
attributed differently, open an issue or contact the maintainer. No justification needed.

What will happen: the content is removed from this repository and from
burnoutaholics.com, and the change ships with the next build. Removal is a rule in the
extraction tooling, not a manual edit, so it survives every future rebuild rather than
reappearing the next time the archive is regenerated.

What cannot be promised: this is a public repository under an open licence, so copies may
exist in forks, clones and web archives such as the Wayback Machine. Those are outside the
maintainer's control. Requests to third parties have to go to whoever runs them.

This is stated plainly rather than left vague, because a takedown route that quietly
overpromises is worse than one with honest limits.

### What is already excluded

Personal data was stripped before publication rather than left for individuals to chase:

- **No email addresses or IP addresses** from the database. Every comment carried an IP and
  349 carried an email; those columns are dropped at extraction and never written to disk.
- **No passwords or password hashes**, and no user account records.
- **No private messages.** The site's 3,608 member-to-member messages are not published.
- **Personal email addresses typed into posts** are redacted; only organisational addresses
  (Criterion, EA, Microsoft, the site's own) remain.
- **One page removed entirely** — a "spammer directory" that paired 86 email addresses and
  31 IP addresses with public accusations.

What remains is usernames and the posts people chose to publish on a public website.

archive, published on the understanding that it documents and celebrates the games rather

does not extend to it.
