Criterion Games have announced that all of the Car Packs will be available for PC users to purchase from today.

The Legendary cars, The Toys and the Boost specials will all be available on the same day, according to the news story at Criterion Games.com.

The story goes on to say that the cars will only be available to purchase as collections, so the PC users will not be able to buy individual cars in the same way that console gamers can.

There was no announcement as to how much the collections will be, but presumably they will be at the same price points as the console equivalents.

For more information check out the news pages at www.criteriongames.com
