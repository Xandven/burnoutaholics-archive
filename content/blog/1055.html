Yesterday Alex Ward intervened and posted a comment at the Criterion Games forum which helps to close the divide in the community. It seems that either the Elite posters were never going to get an exclusive car, but were given a beta but it was worded badly. Or that Criterion have backtracked due to the unfairness of it all and the forum riot that they had!

Either way, I am glad the car is going to be available for everybody soon, I just hope that it isn't as fast as cars with boost. If the fastest car in the game is a boostless car Paradise online isn't going to look like a Burnout game anymore!

Here is the bulk of Alex's message:

We should have used the term 'closed beta' rather than 'for Elites.'

Personally, it's a term that doesn't sit well with me. The game is for everyone, all players, all abilties.

To me, everyone is welcome in Paradise.

For those of you totally into status, items and wanting to feel superior - then that's great. But Burnout is evolving, changing, moving forwards.

For some of you, you get it, you embrace the changes, you go where we're going.

For some of you, you will always want something which we are sadly unable to provide, you won't embrace our changes, you won't come with us when we move forward.

Forward to me means new players, new experiences, more fun, more laughter, more great online play.

Probably won't be as competitive as it has been, probably won't be about elites, probably won't be a place where you can come and feel superior.

New vehicles are going to come and everyone is going to get them.

Microsoft players, don't worry you will get stuff fast. No-one will miss out on anything.

If you buy the game on 'graduation day' then you still get the stuff and all the stuff that comes previously. There is no need for anyone to worry.

We hope you all continue to enjoy the new content we released free of charge last week.

Coming really soon now are the motorcycles and we're putting the final touches to them now. They are simple and easy to ride, and riding around Paradise City at both day and night is fun and entertainining. I especially like seeing the stars coming out in the early evening sky. Even the most hardcore fans will be surprised at just how much of a difference this simple change makes to the game.

As I said to someone in the office just yesterday - 'Burnout is what it is TODAY - and that's a live connected experience where you come to share fun driving experiences online.' For us, it's about moving forwards.

We hope that you will contine with us on this journey.

Hope you enjoy reading about the announcements at E3 as much as we do - and maybe see you online..

Alex
