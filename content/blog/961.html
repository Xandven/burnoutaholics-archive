Criterion Games announced last week that there was going to be another big announcement on the Podcast on Friday, this filled me with dread cos I don't like surprises... the last announcement they made was for a car with no boost which has a really fast top speed.. surely this big heavy car can't be faster then an aerodynamic PCPD with boost???  The big announcement tho was for Motorbikes and night time which I am looking forward to trying out.

The new big announcement tho has me worried, it has been put back a week to next Friday, and Criterion have invited us to guess what the announcement might be. According to Crash FM podcast 30 none of us have guessed correctly yet.

So it wont be a replay mode, or big rigs. It wont be snow or boats. Most of the guesses have been off the wall, but some of them are a bit more realistic. What worries me most tho is the Burnout Team seem to want to shock us, so what are they planning? is it so removed from Burnout that nobody will EVER guess what it is??

What is your best guess?💡
