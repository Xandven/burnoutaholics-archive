Members of BurnoutAholics.com get awarded Addiction Points when they contribute to the site. The Addiction Points indicate how much a user has contributed either by adding content like blogs, comments, articles or by donating money to the site.

Currently points can be earned as follows:

  • 3 points for writing an entry in your blog
  • 1 point for comments
  • 1 point for posting a forum topic or a comment
  • 1 point for a poll that gets published on the site
  • 5 points for one Question and Answer in a FAQ

You can also earn from 10 – 100 points for writing an article that gets posted on the front page of BurnoutAholics.com. Also Xandu and ZombieTron can choose to award points to users that contribute in a special way.
