WOO! it downloaded in time for my lunch break!! 🙂

I just spent the last 45 minutes freeburning with Suffur. The demo does feel a little small on first play, I was crashing into a lot of road blocks!

I like how it says that the road will re-open in January 2008.

There also doesn't seem to be any Showtime mode in the demo?

I haven't tried any of the events yet, just did one of the online challenges. But time was restricted and I kinda find it hard to stop freeburning because I think its so much fun just tearing around the city and finding smashes and billboards and ramps to jump from.

I have had a couple of messages from people saying they are not too impressed. But I don't see how you can not be impressed with this game. The City is beautiful, it runs so smoothly and the sense of speed even in the first car in the game is FAST! But, it is different from Revenge and I think it definatly takes a bit of getting used to for all of the changes. Like using the D-pad to see your friends and send invites.

Also, I did manage to fall through the floor once on 4th and Webster after hitting a road block!! haha its still Burnout!! 😃

I LOVE PARADISE!! and I hope you all grow to love it as much as I do!!
