I haven't been to the cinema this much in ages!! 🎬

So far this Summer we have seen pretty much all of the big blockbusters (except Mamma Mia and Sex and the City) So, I thought I would give a mini rundown and super quick mini review (as its my lunch break). WARNING MAY CONTAIN SPOILERS!

Iron Man -  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Awesome movie and Robert Downey Jr was PERFECT! Loved every minute and the Avengers teaser was the icing on the cake! 🎂

The Incredible Hulk - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I was a bit worried about Ed Norton as the Hulk, but he was brill! and they even tipped a hat to the 70's tv show. They had a cameo from Lou Ferrigno as well as the obligatory Stan Lee cameo. And another Avengers teaser too!!! Can't wait for that movie!!!

Wanted - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

We saw the trailer to this when we went to see Iron Man and The Incredible Hulk and it looked like a super cool movie. It didn't dissapoint (can't believe I forgot about it) Coolness in many ways, especially the clever use of a computer keyboard! 💻

The Forbidden Kingdon - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Movie history is made! Seeing Jackie Chan and Jet Li fight it out in the temple was fantastic! If (like me) you used to watch Monkey Magic you should love this movie! Loads of martial art legends in the opening credits, how many can you name? Beautiful movie, I loved it!

Hancock - ⭐⭐⭐

OK I didn't love this one so much, it was OK, entertaining enough and did have me giggling a few times. I also really enjoyed Jason Bateman's performance.  Good fun, but thats about it.

HellBoy II: The Golden Army - ⭐⭐⭐⭐

I didn't know Luke Goss was in this until the credits rolled! doh! He was brilliant! I used to like Bros, I'm able to admit it! 😳 He's a much better actor than he was a musician!! Cool movie, cool monsters, scary bits and funny bits, everything you want from a HellBoy movie! and I am really glad I closed my eyes and covered my ears when the trailer was on, I hate that they show too much in the trailers!

Batman: The Dark Knight - ⭐⭐⭐⭐

I think I hyped this in my head too much to give it 5 stars. I really liked it but there were a few bits I found hard to follow, especially some of the action sequences. And there just wasn't enough Batman in it for me. I love Christian Bale, but he just isn't in this movie that much?! Heath Ledger was as good as everybody says, Aaron Eckhart, Morgan Freeman and Michael Caine were all great too, but something was missing for me... maybe the Bat Cave?? or maybe I just had a tinge of sadness over Heath Ledger's untimely death or maybe too many people were talking through the movie, and I hate that! I think I might have to rewatch this one... I will be getting it on DVD anyway to add to my Christian Bale collection! 😉

I feel like I have missed one.. so I might come back and edit this later.

Roll on Summer '09!!
