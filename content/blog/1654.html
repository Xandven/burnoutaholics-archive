moving to Norway from the UK was pretty easy, even tho I haven't got my removals yet... I am really hoping everything will be delivered this week as I am missing my stuff, and am especially looking forward to getting the paintings that I packed and putting them up on our bare walls.

Immigration on the other hand is turning out to be a lot more complicated than we first thought. Having read all the relevant web pages before I left the UK it seemed like it would be a relatively straight forward process. Register with the police station and wait... but... we have today recieved a letter from the immigration authorities in Norway requesting that I get a certificate of no impediment. A what??? I had to google it as I had never heard of it before, turns out it is a certificate that states that I am not married to somebody else and I will have to contact the British consulate in Norway to get one, at a cost!

oh well, at least it gives me something to do tomorrow! 🙂
