This is a challenge to those of you who have ever played NFS:HP2 (Need For Speed: Hot Pursuit 2) on either Xbox or PS2.  I've played it on PS2 and PC (it's not the same on PC...), and it's one of my favorite racing games of all time.

Anyway, I went back today and, using a McLaren F1 on Coastal Parklands (the easiest track), recorded a clean lap of 1:28.17.  My best lap was 1:21.91.  For a 4 lap event, my best time was 5:46.99 [Lap 1) 1:34.81, Lap 2) 1:24.24, Lap 3) 1:21.91, Lap 4) 1:26.03].

My challenge is for those of you who still have this game to attempt to get a fast clean lap, fastest lap, and fastest time in a 4 lap event.  You can even go a step further than I did and attempt an 8 lap race! Feel free to post your results, whether you beat my times or not... I think it'll be difficult, but a low 1:21 may be possible. Good luck! 😃
