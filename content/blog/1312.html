OK so here is a quick list of the top things that I Love about Burnout, there are more things I love, but these are the main ones:

1) Boost! Boost! Boost!

I love the boost system Burnout style! I love driving dangerously to earn boost. Weaving in and out of oncoming traffic, over bumps and jumps and drifting all the way!

2) The Best Track Designs!

The Burnout series has the absolute best track designs of any racing series ever! (ok I haven't played them all, but this is still a FACT!)

From Gridlock to Big Surf Shores, Crystal Summit to Winter City, Island Paradise to Central Route, I love all of the tracks. The corners perfect for drifting, the straights perfect for flat out boosting and battling with your opponents. The ramps, bumps & jumps all placed perfectly for great racing action! The short-cuts and alternate routes to discover. The wonderful scenery and the beautiful sunsets, "look at the sky!!" 🙂

3) Battle Racing!

There is no sweeter feeling then slamming your opponent into a wall on the last corner to take 1st place! Getting takedowns until you get Boost Locked! Driving defensively and beating 5 reds in a game of cat and mouse!

This is why I love Burnout! ❤️
